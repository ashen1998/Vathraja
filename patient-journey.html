What to expect

Your Journey, from First Consultation to Review

Every pathway at Vathraja Facial Chambers follows the same shape: assess, explain, confirm suitability, treat where appropriate, and review. You should never be unsure about what happens next.

Book a Consultation Inside a skin consultation
The pathway

Five stages, in order

No stage is skipped. If a stage raises something that needs addressing, we pause there rather than move on.

Consultation first, safety first. Treatment only follows once suitability has been confirmed clinically.

  1. 01

    Stage one

    Consultation

    You meet the clinician responsible for your pathway. We discuss what brought you in, what you would like to change, and what you have already tried. This is a conversation, not a sales appointment — and it is private and unhurried.

    No obligation to proceed

  2. 02

    Stage two

    Assessment and diagnosis

    Your clinician examines your skin or facial structure and reviews your medical history, medications, allergies, and previous treatments. Where a concern has a diagnosis, you are told what it appears to be and what is contributing to it.

    You are told what we found

  3. 03

    Stage three

    Treatment planning

    Suitable options are explained together with what they may support, what they will not change, the number of sessions likely to be involved, the risks, and the aftercare required. You are given time to consider — decisions are not asked for on the spot.

    Time to think it over

  4. 04

    Stage four

    Treatment

    Where you decide to proceed and the treatment remains clinically appropriate on the day, it is carried out by the responsible clinician. If your skin or circumstances have changed since planning, we reassess before starting.

    Reassessed on the day

  5. 05

    Stage five

    Aftercare and follow-up

    You leave with written aftercare and a point of contact. Where a review is recommended, it is scheduled, and the plan is adjusted based on how you actually responded rather than how you were expected to.

    Written aftercare, every time

Throughout

What stays constant at every stage

—

A named clinician

You know who is responsible for your care, and you see the same clinician through the pathway wherever possible.

—

Plain explanations

Clinical reasoning, described in language you can act on, including the limits of what a treatment may achieve.

—

No pressure

Time to think, and no obligation to proceed. A consultation that ends in “not yet” is a legitimate outcome.

—

Documented aftercare

Written guidance for anything carried out, plus a contact point if something concerns you afterwards.

Timing

How long does it take?

That depends entirely on the concern. Some plans are a single appointment with a review; others run over months. What we can do is set out an honest, indicative timeline at planning, and tell you if it changes.

If you are working towards a wedding or a milestone event, tell us the date at the first appointment. Planning backwards from a fixed date is very different from planning forwards from today.

Planning for a big day

Questions worth asking us

  • What exactly are you assessing, and what did you find?
  • What would happen if I did nothing?
  • What are the alternatives to what you are proposing?
  • What does this treatment not change?
  • How will we know whether it is working?
  • What would make you advise against proceeding?
