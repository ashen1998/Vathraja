The first appointment

Start with a Professional Skin Consultation

A consultation helps us understand your skin before recommending treatment. During this session, we discuss your concerns, examine your skin condition, review relevant medical and skin care history, and explain suitable options.

Book a Skin Consultation What we review
The checklist

What we review during consultation

A consultation is a structured clinical conversation. These are the areas we work through, in roughly this order.

01

Your main skin or facial concerns

What brought you in, how long it has been present, whether it fluctuates, and what makes it better or worse.

02

Desired results and expectations

What you would like to see change, and how that compares with what is realistically achievable for your skin.

03

Skin type and condition

An examination of your skin as it is on the day — type, sensitivity, barrier condition, and any active concerns.

04

Medical history

Relevant conditions, allergies, medications, and previous treatments, including anything carried out elsewhere.

05

Current skincare and lifestyle

The products you use, how often, and the lifestyle factors — sun exposure, travel, stress, sleep — that affect your skin.

06

Recommended options

Suitable options, the number of sessions that may be involved, aftercare requirements, and expected results.

Preparing

How to get the most from your appointment

None of this is essential — come as you are if it is easier. But if you have time, these few things make the assessment more useful.

  • Bring your current skincare products, or photograph the labels
  • Note the names of any medications you take regularly
  • Come with clean skin if convenient, or without heavy makeup
  • Bring details of previous treatments and roughly when they were done
  • Write down your questions beforehand — they are easy to forget in the room

What you leave with

  • A clear explanation of what we found
  • The options that may help, and those that will not
  • An indicative plan, including sessions and timelines
  • Aftercare requirements for anything proposed
  • Time to think it over — you are never asked to decide on the spot

A consultation does not commit you to treatment. Treatment outcomes vary by individual and suitability is confirmed only after clinical assessment.
