Treatment approach

Structured courses, honest reviews

Most cosmetic skin treatments work as a course rather than a single visit. We set out how many sessions may be involved, at what spacing, and what should be visible along the way — so progress can be judged against something concrete.

At review, if a course is not producing the expected response, we say so and change the plan. Continuing a treatment that is not working is not a service to you.

What you can expect

  • A doctor-led assessment before your first treatment
  • Clear, written aftercare after every session
  • A defined number of sessions and spacing, agreed in advance
  • Review points where progress is assessed honestly
  • Referral within Vathraja where a concern needs specialist input
  • No pressure to extend a course that is not helping

A note on safety and suitability

Cosmetic treatments still carry risks, and suitability depends on your skin, your medical history, and what has been done recently. Assessment happens before every course, and again if your circumstances change.

Treatment outcomes vary by individual and require professional consultation. Where a treatment is not appropriate for you, we will explain why and what the alternatives are.
