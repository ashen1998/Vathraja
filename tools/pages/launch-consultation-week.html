What launch week is

An introduction to how we work

Launch Consultation Week is an opportunity to meet the clinicians, see the chambers, and have your concerns assessed properly — without any expectation that you proceed to treatment.

A full consultation

A proper assessment with a consultant or doctor — not a shortened introductory session or a product demonstration.

A written plan

If treatment is appropriate, you leave with the options, sessions, timelines, and aftercare set out clearly.

No pressure to proceed

There is no obligation attached to a launch consultation. Take the plan away and decide in your own time.

Who it is for

Worth booking if you are

  • Unsure whether your concern is a dermatology question or an aesthetic one
  • Considering injectables and want an anatomy-led opinion on suitability first
  • Managing acne, pigmentation, sensitivity, or scarring that has not settled
  • Planning ahead for a wedding or milestone event
  • Looking for a second opinion on a plan proposed elsewhere

Practical details

  • Where: Vathraja Facial Chambers, One Galle Face, Colombo
  • Format: Private, appointment-only consultation
  • With: A consultant or doctor, depending on your concern
  • Bring: Current skincare products and a list of medications

Treatment outcomes vary by individual and require professional consultation. Suitability for any procedure is confirmed only after clinical assessment.
