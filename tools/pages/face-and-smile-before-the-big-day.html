Why plan early

Good outcomes need runway, not urgency

Smile alignment takes months. Skin concerns such as pigmentation, acne, or scarring improve gradually. Facial aesthetic treatments need time to settle and a review before a repeat.

Starting early gives room for all of that — and, importantly, room to stop if something is not progressing as hoped. Starting late narrows the options, and sometimes the right advice is to wait until after the event.

We will not recommend treatment close to a date where there is insufficient time for settling and review. That is a safety position, not a scheduling preference.

What a combined plan can cover

  • Aligner assessment and smile planning with Danthaja Aligner Center
  • A dermatology consultation for skin concerns that need lead time
  • Facial aesthetics planning, where clinically appropriate
  • A skin-health routine you can maintain in the months leading up
  • Sequencing, so treatments do not conflict with one another
  • A realistic calendar, with review points built in

Planning backwards from the date

Every plan is individual. This is the general shape of a combined face and smile timeline, agreed and adjusted at consultation.

01

12–9 months out

First consultations. Aligner assessment, skin assessment, and an agreed sequence with realistic expectations.

02

9–4 months out

The working phase. Aligner treatment progresses; skin plans run their course with reviews along the way.

03

4–2 months out

Refinement and review. Any facial aesthetics planning is considered here, with time to settle and reassess.

04

Final weeks

Maintenance only. No new or stronger treatments — the focus shifts to keeping your skin calm and protected.
