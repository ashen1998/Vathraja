What we assess

Suitability is decided before technique

An injectable treatment is a medical procedure performed in an anatomically complex area. The assessment is what makes it a considered decision rather than a purchase.

Facial structure

Underlying bone, soft tissue, and the anatomy of the treatment area — assessed by a clinician with surgical training in the facial region.

Your goals

What you want to change and why, including whether an injectable is the appropriate route to that goal at all.

Medical history

Conditions, medications, allergies, autoimmune history, pregnancy or breastfeeding, and anything affecting healing or bleeding.

Previous injectable treatment

What was used, where, when, and how you responded — including treatment carried out elsewhere.

Risks and complications

The recognised risks of the specific procedure, how they are managed, and what would require urgent contact.

Expected outcome and duration

When a change may become visible, how long it may last, and what maintenance would realistically involve.

Treatment approach

Conservative, reviewed, and reversible where possible

Injectable treatment at Vathraja is led by a Consultant Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon. Planning considers the whole face rather than a single point, and a conservative starting dose with a planned review is usually preferred to treating everything at once.

Not every request is appropriate. Where an injectable is unlikely to achieve what you are hoping for, where the anatomy carries elevated risk, or where another approach would serve you better, we will tell you that directly.

Before anything is agreed, you will know

  • Whether you are a suitable candidate, and why
  • What the treatment can realistically change — and what it cannot
  • The recognised risks and side effects for that specific area
  • What the first 48 hours and first two weeks typically involve
  • When a review appointment is scheduled
  • What to do, and who to contact, if something concerns you

Safety first

An injectable treatment is a medical decision

Suitability for any injectable procedure can only be confirmed after a professional consultation and clinical assessment. Outcomes vary between individuals, and no result is guaranteed.
