Consultant-led dermatology

Healthy Skin Begins with the Right Diagnosis

Meet with a specialist to understand acne, pigmentation, sensitivity, scars, ageing concerns, and long-term skin health before choosing treatment.

Book a Dermatology Consultation What happens at a skin consultation
What we assess

A skin concern is a clinical question first

Two people with the same visible concern can need entirely different management. The consultation exists to tell those situations apart.

Acne and acne-prone skin

Type, distribution, severity, contributing factors, and scarring risk — including when a medical rather than cosmetic approach is appropriate.

Pigmentation and uneven tone

Pigment concerns behave differently depending on cause and depth, and sun exposure history matters as much as the treatment chosen.

Sensitivity and barrier concerns

Reactive, irritated, or over-treated skin often needs settling before anything active is introduced.

Scarring and texture

Scar type determines what may realistically help, and improvement is usually gradual rather than immediate.

Skin ageing

Texture, laxity, pigment, and volume change at different rates. Understanding which is driving your concern shapes the plan.

Long-term skin health

Maintenance, sun protection, and a routine you can realistically keep — the part that sustains any result.

Treatment approach

Diagnosis, then a plan you can follow

Your consultant dermatologist examines your skin, reviews your history, and explains what is happening before discussing options. Where treatment is appropriate, it is set out as a plan — not a single appointment.

Some concerns respond to in-clinic treatment, some to medical management, and some to a change in routine. Many need a combination, and most need time.

A dermatology plan typically covers

  • What your skin concern appears to be, and what is contributing to it
  • Which options may help, and which are unlikely to
  • How many sessions or how long a course may take
  • Home care: what to use, what to pause, and what to avoid
  • Sun protection appropriate to the treatment
  • When to return for review, and what we will look for
Safety and suitability

Checked before anything is recommended

Skin treatments interact with medication, medical conditions, pregnancy, recent procedures, and the products you already use. Your clinician reviews all of it before confirming that an option is appropriate for you.

Our safety and aftercare approach

We review before we treat

Medical history, medications, allergies, pregnancy, and recent procedures — every time, not just at the first visit.

Results vary, and we say so

Some concerns improve substantially, some partially, and some need ongoing management rather than a one-off treatment.

We refer when we should

Where a concern needs input beyond our scope, we will tell you plainly and refer you appropriately.
