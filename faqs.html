Safety and suitability

Suitability and aftercare

If in doubt, ask before you book — particularly about medication, pregnancy, or recent procedures.

Safety and aftercare in full

Several things: active skin infection or irritation, certain medical conditions or medications, pregnancy and breastfeeding for some treatments, recent procedures, significant recent sun exposure, or expectations that the treatment could not realistically meet.

Contact the clinic. Mild redness or tightness can be expected after some treatments, but anything unexpected, worsening, or painful should be reviewed rather than managed at home.
